SECTION 1: OVERVIEW OF UPKSK SERVICES & MEDCUBE INTEGRATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
UPKSK (Uttar Pradesh ke Swasthya Kendra) is a Government of UP initiative providing centralized information about public health facilities.
*NEW INTEGRATION*: Medcube Diagnostic & Telemedicine Hubs are now active. Medcube provides rapid AI-assisted diagnostics, pathology, advanced imaging (MRI/CT), and immediate high-definition tele-consultations with top specialists across the state.
Helpline Numbers:
- UPKSK Health Helpline: 1800-180-5145 (Toll-Free, 24x7)
- Ambulance (108): Dial 108 for emergency ambulance
- Emergency: 112

SECTION 2: SPECIALIST CATEGORIES AND SYMPTOMS GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 ORTHOPEDICS (Haddi Rog Vibhag)
Specialist: Orthopedist / Orthopedic Surgeon
Treats: Bone fractures, joint pain, ligament tears, dislocations, arthritis, back pain, spinal problems, sports injuries, osteoporosis.
Common Symptoms: Severe bone pain after injury, inability to move a limb, visible deformity, swelling near joints, chronic joint pain.

2.2 GENERAL MEDICINE (Samanya Chikitsa)
Specialist: General Physician / Medicine Specialist
Treats: Fever, cold, cough, diabetes, hypertension, thyroid disorders, respiratory infections, gastrointestinal issues, malaria, dengue.
Common Symptoms: Prolonged fever, fatigue, body ache, headache, nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, dizziness.

2.3 GENERAL SURGERY (Samanya Shalya Chikitsa)
Specialist: General Surgeon
Treats: Hernia, appendicitis, gallbladder stones, piles (bawasir), fistula, abscess drainage, minor tumor removal, wound management.
Common Symptoms: Severe abdominal pain, lump in groin area, bleeding from rectum, non-healing wounds, swollen painful area.

2.4 GYNECOLOGY & OBSTETRICS (Stree Rog & Prasuti)
Specialist: Gynecologist / Obstetrician
Treats: Pregnancy care, normal/cesarean delivery, menstrual disorders, PCOS, infertility, uterine fibroids, ovarian cysts.
Common Symptoms: Missed periods, pregnancy-related issues, heavy or irregular bleeding, pelvic pain.

2.5 PEDIATRICS (Bal Rog)
Specialist: Pediatrician / Child Specialist
Treats: Childhood illnesses, newborn care, vaccination, childhood infections, growth issues, childhood asthma, malnutrition.
Common Symptoms: Child with high fever, difficulty breathing, persistent vomiting/diarrhea, rash, poor feeding.

2.6 NEUROLOGY & CARDIOLOGY (Mastishk aur Hriday Rog)
Specialist: Neurologist / Cardiologist
Treats: Heart attack, stroke, epilepsy/seizures, chest pain, heart failure, paralysis, nerve disorders.
Common Symptoms: Chest pain/tightness (EMERGENCY - call 108), sudden weakness on one side (stroke), seizures, severe headache, breathlessness.

SECTION 6: EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS FOR CITIZENS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1 When to Call 108 (Emergency Ambulance):
- Chest pain or suspected heart attack
- Stroke symptoms (sudden face drooping, arm weakness, speech difficulty)
- Severe road accident or trauma
- Heavy uncontrolled bleeding
- Unconsciousness or seizures

6.2 Government Health Schemes:
- Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY): Free treatment up to INR 5,00,000 per family per year at empanelled hospitals.
- Mukhyamantri Jan Arogya Yojana: Additional coverage for UP residents.
