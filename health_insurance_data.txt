SECTION 1: OVERVIEW OF UPKSK SERVICES
--------------------------------------------------------------------
UPKSK (Uttar Pradesh ke Swasthya Kendra) is a Government of Uttar
Pradesh initiative providing centralized information about public
health facilities across the state. This directory helps citizens
find the right hospital and specialist doctor based on their medical
needs. All listed facilities are government hospitals providing
affordable or free treatment under various government health schemes
including Ayushman Bharat and Mukhyamantri Jan Arogya Yojana.

Helpline Numbers:
- UPKSK Health Helpline: 1800-180-5145 (Toll-Free, 24x7)
- Ambulance (108): Dial 108 for emergency ambulance
- National Health Helpline: 104
- Emergency: 112

SECTION 2: SPECIALIST CATEGORIES AND SYMPTOMS GUIDE
--------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 ORTHOPEDICS (Haddi Rog Vibhag)
Specialist: Orthopedist / Orthopedic Surgeon
Treats: Bone fractures, joint pain, ligament tears, dislocations,
arthritis, back pain, spinal problems, sports injuries, bone
infections, osteoporosis, knee replacement, hip replacement.
Common Symptoms: Severe bone pain after injury, inability to move
a limb, visible deformity, swelling near joints, persistent back
pain, clicking/locking joints, difficulty walking.
When to visit: Any injury involving bones or joints, fall from
height, road accident with limb injury, chronic joint pain.

2.2 GENERAL MEDICINE (Samanya Chikitsa)
Specialist: General Physician / Medicine Specialist
Treats: Fever, cold, cough, diabetes, hypertension, thyroid
disorders, respiratory infections, gastrointestinal issues,
general weakness, infectious diseases, malaria, dengue, typhoid.
Common Symptoms: Prolonged fever, fatigue, body ache, headache,
nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, dizziness, high/low blood pressure.
When to visit: Any general illness, first consultation for
undiagnosed problems, chronic disease management.

2.3 GENERAL SURGERY (Samanya Shalya Chikitsa)
Specialist: General Surgeon
Treats: Hernia, appendicitis, gallbladder stones, piles (bawasir),
fistula, abscess drainage, minor tumor removal, wound management,
trauma cases, intestinal obstruction.
Common Symptoms: Severe abdominal pain, lump in groin area,
bleeding from rectum, non-healing wounds, swollen painful area.
When to visit: Acute abdominal pain, any condition needing surgery.

2.4 GYNECOLOGY & OBSTETRICS (Stree Rog & Prasuti)
Specialist: Gynecologist / Obstetrician
Treats: Pregnancy care, normal and cesarean delivery, menstrual
disorders, PCOS, infertility, uterine fibroids, ovarian cysts,
high-risk pregnancy management, family planning.
Common Symptoms: Missed periods, pregnancy-related issues, heavy
or irregular bleeding, pelvic pain, pregnancy complications.
When to visit: Pregnancy checkups, delivery, any female
reproductive health issues, family planning counseling.

2.5 PEDIATRICS (Bal Rog)
Specialist: Pediatrician / Child Specialist
Treats: All childhood illnesses, newborn care, vaccination,
childhood infections, growth and development issues, childhood
asthma, malnutrition, diarrhea in children, neonatal jaundice.
Common Symptoms: Child with high fever, difficulty breathing,
persistent vomiting/diarrhea, rash, poor feeding, low weight.
When to visit: Any illness in children aged 0-18 years.

2.6 ENT (Kaan-Naak-Gala)
Specialist: ENT Specialist / Otolaryngologist
Treats: Ear infections, hearing loss, tonsillitis, sinusitis,
nasal polyps, deviated septum, throat infections, voice disorders,
vertigo/dizziness related to ear, foreign body in ear/nose/throat.
Common Symptoms: Ear pain, hearing difficulty, blocked nose,
throat pain, snoring, nosebleed, ringing in ears (tinnitus).
When to visit: Persistent ear/nose/throat problems, hearing issues.

2.7 OPHTHALMOLOGY (Netra Rog)
Specialist: Eye Specialist / Ophthalmologist
Treats: Cataract, glaucoma, refractive errors (spectacle number),
eye infections (conjunctivitis), diabetic retinopathy, squint,
retinal detachment, corneal problems, eye injuries.
Common Symptoms: Blurred vision, eye pain, redness, watering,
sudden vision loss, double vision, eye injury, floaters.
When to visit: Any vision problems, eye injury, routine eye checkup.

2.8 DERMATOLOGY (Charm Rog)
Specialist: Dermatologist / Skin Specialist
Treats: Skin infections, eczema, psoriasis, acne, fungal infections,
allergic skin reactions, vitiligo, skin cancer screening, hair loss,
nail disorders, sexually transmitted skin infections, leprosy.
Common Symptoms: Persistent rash, itching, skin discoloration,
hair loss, non-healing skin sore, unusual mole changes.
When to visit: Any persistent skin, hair, or nail problem.

2.9 CARDIOLOGY (Hriday Rog)
Specialist: Cardiologist / Heart Specialist
Treats: Heart attack, chest pain, heart failure, arrhythmia,
valve diseases, coronary artery disease, congenital heart defects,
hypertension management, cardiac rehabilitation.
Common Symptoms: Chest pain/tightness, breathlessness on exertion,
palpitations, fainting, swelling in legs, persistent fatigue.
When to visit: Chest pain (EMERGENCY — call 108), breathlessness,
known heart condition follow-up.

2.10 NEUROLOGY (Mastishk Rog)
Specialist: Neurologist / Brain Specialist
Treats: Stroke, epilepsy/seizures, migraine, Parkinson's disease,
paralysis, nerve disorders, multiple sclerosis, brain tumors,
neuropathy, movement disorders, dementia/memory loss.
Common Symptoms: Sudden weakness on one side (stroke — EMERGENCY),
seizures, severe headache, numbness/tingling, tremors, memory loss.
When to visit: Sudden one-sided weakness (EMERGENCY — call 108),
recurring seizures, chronic headaches, neurological symptoms.

2.11 PSYCHIATRY (Mansik Rog)
Specialist: Psychiatrist / Mental Health Specialist
Treats: Depression, anxiety, schizophrenia, bipolar disorder,
substance abuse/addiction, sleep disorders, OCD, PTSD, behavioral
problems in children, suicidal tendencies, stress-related disorders.
Common Symptoms: Persistent sadness, excessive worry, hearing
voices, substance addiction, insomnia, social withdrawal.
When to visit: Any mental health concern. Crisis helpline: iCALL
9152987821 or Vandrevala Foundation 1860-2662-345.

2.12 DENTAL (Dant Chikitsa)
Specialist: Dentist / Dental Surgeon
Treats: Tooth decay, gum disease, tooth extraction, root canal,
dental abscess, jaw pain, orthodontic issues, dental trauma.
Common Symptoms: Toothache, bleeding gums, swollen jaw, broken
tooth, difficulty chewing, bad breath.
When to visit: Any tooth or gum problem, dental injury.

2.13 UROLOGY (Mutraroga)
Specialist: Urologist
Treats: Kidney stones, urinary tract infections, prostate problems,
bladder disorders, kidney disorders, male infertility, urinary
incontinence, kidney failure management.
Common Symptoms: Painful urination, blood in urine, frequent
urination, flank pain, difficulty passing urine, kidney stone pain.
When to visit: Severe flank/kidney pain, blood in urine, urinary
problems.

SECTION 8: EMERGENCY PROTOCOLS FOR CITIZENS
--------------------------------------------------------------------
8.1 When to Call 108 (Emergency Ambulance):
- Chest pain or suspected heart attack
- Stroke symptoms (sudden face drooping, arm weakness, speech
  difficulty) — Remember F.A.S.T.
- Severe road accident or trauma
- Difficulty breathing or choking
- Heavy uncontrolled bleeding
- Unconsciousness or seizures
- Severe burns
- Snake bite or animal bite
- Poisoning or drug overdose

8.2 What to Carry to the Hospital:
- Aadhaar Card or any government photo ID
- Ayushman Bharat Golden Card (if available)
- Previous medical records and prescriptions
- BPL card (if applicable, for free treatment)
- Health insurance card (if any)

8.3 Government Health Schemes:
- Ayushman Bharat (PMJAY): Free treatment up to INR 5,00,000 per
  family per year at empanelled hospitals. Eligibility: SECC 2011
  listed families. Apply at nearest CSC or Ayushman Mitra.
- Mukhyamantri Jan Arogya Yojana: Additional coverage for UP
  residents not covered under PMJAY.
- Janani Suraksha Yojana: Free institutional delivery and cash
  incentive for pregnant women.
- Rashtriya Bal Swasthya Karyakram: Free health screening and
  treatment for children 0-18 years.

SECTION 9: OPD BOOKING AND GENERAL INFORMATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------
Most government hospitals offer walk-in OPD registration. Some
hospitals also provide online appointment booking:
- RMLIMS Lucknow: Book at drrmlimsonline.in
- KGMU Lucknow: Walk-in registration starts 7:30 AM
- BHU Varanasi: Walk-in registration starts 7:00 AM

OPD Registration Fee: INR 10 at most government hospitals.
BPL card holders: Free registration.

Note: OPD is generally available Monday to Saturday. Emergency
services are available 24x7 at all listed hospitals. Sunday OPD
is usually closed except for emergency and casualty departments.
